title: Cloud Native Parity & Performance
description: To ensure Arm64 achieves full platform parity with x86 across cloud-native workloads. This group collaborates on native CI pipelines, performance regression testing, and maintainer enablement to establish Arm64 as a tier-1 architecture.

## Joining
  To join the Working Group, sign and return the CoreCollective Membership
Agreement found <a href="https://corecollective.dev/join/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">here.</a>
  Once the free membership has been fully signed, you'll be able to register
and sign into the <a href="https://hub.corecollective.dev/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">CoreCollective hub</a> and join any desired Working Groups.
Subscriptions are restricted to company email domains of current members.
