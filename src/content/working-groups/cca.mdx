## Joining
  To join the Working Group, sign and return the CoreCollective Membership
Agreement found <a href="https://corecollective.dev/join/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">here.</a> Membership is free.
  Once the free membership has been fully signed, you'll be able to register
and sign into the <a href="https://hub.corecollective.dev/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">CoreCollective hub</a> and join any desired Working Groups.
Subscriptions are restricted to company email domains of current members.

## Participate in meetings
  Please note that our Confidential Compute meetings are open to all. They take place bi-weekly on Mondays.
  
  Every time there are two meetings with the same agenda to accomodate for different time zones.
