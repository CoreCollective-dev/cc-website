The purpose of the CoreCollective UEFI Architecture working group is to
propose, collaborate, align and prioritize collaborative efforts in the UEFI Architecture.

The vision is to also:

* Focus on developing standards to increase multi-domain compatibility
* Bridging business-driven needs with technology-driven solutions
* Collaborating on common goals with other non-x86 architectures, like RISC-V

## Joining

If your company is not yet a CoreCollective member, have your company sign and return the 
<a href="/join/" target="_blank">CoreCollective Membership Agreement found here</a>. Membership is free. 
Once the membership agreement has been fully signed, you’ll be able to register and sign into the <a href="https://hub.corecollective.dev/" target="_blank">CoreCollective hub</a> 
and join any desired Working Groups.
